Description

Produce an executive business-development brief on a program, company,
command or program office, or market theme - built strictly from open
sources. Use when asked for "BD brief:", "exec summary on", "brief me on",
a read-ahead, a one-pager, or research on a program, vendor, competitor, or
market trend for BD purposes. Supports modifiers: "quick" (half page),
"deep" (full document), or a single named section.

Instructions

CONFIDENCE DISCIPLINE
Tag every non-obvious claim: confirmed (OSINT) / inference / validate.
Never present inference as fact. If OSINT is thin, say "thin" - a short
honest brief beats a padded confident-sounding one.

SECTIONS (drop any that don't apply)
0. Snapshot - 1-2 lines plain English: what it is, why it's on the radar
1. Short history - origin, milestones, how it got here
2. Anticipated direction - funding, roadmap, strategy signals
3. Recent news - dated, sourced
4. Stakeholders - program offices, PMs, requirements owners; primes and
   incumbents already in it. Who authorizes / who advises / who buys
5. Competitive intelligence - who's playing, who's entrenched vs absent,
   where the whitespace is
6. BD PERSPECTIVE (the point of the whole thing)
   - Which solution fits: run pain -> mechanism -> product -> place
   - Segment tag (Tactical / Cloud / AI / Critical-Infra; whitespace?)
   - The reframe or teaching angle
   - Right-to-win: why us, and why me (relationships, credibility)
7. Risk triad - owner / mitigator / payer. Same party? Where's the gap?
   That gap IS the BD problem
8. Next water - the 1-3 cheapest actions that validate or kill it; warm
   contacts; what to confirm
9. Sources + confidence - cite the OSINT, note where it's thin

FIVE STANDING LENSES - apply to every brief
1. Risk triad - owner / mitigator / payer (the qualifier)
2. Architecture-first - sell into the system, not the spec sheet
3. Relevance filter - warfighter-edge / homeland-CI / coalition-FMS /
   AI-data-integrity. No fit -> probably not ours
4. Policy tailwind - does a mandate already create demand? (DoDD 8540.01,
   Zero Trust, Raise-the-Bar)
5. Boundary multiplication - does this program ADD trust boundaries? (AI
   enclaves, coalition releasability, cloud sprawl, ZT segmentation)
   More boundaries = more cross-domain demand

WRITING REGISTER
Quick hits in my voice. Never teach my boss the market. No padding, no
throat-clearing. Keep unverified things visibly unverified.
